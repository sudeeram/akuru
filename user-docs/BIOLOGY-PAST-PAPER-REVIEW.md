# Review Biology past papers

The imported Biology material is a draft bank for Admin review. Upload completion does not mean a paper or marking scheme has been approved for Students.

1. Sign in as Admin and open **Exam documents** at `/admin/exam-documents`.
2. Select **Biology**. Find the exam session and component. Standard papers are **1B/2B**; regional papers are **1BR/2BR**. Keep their companions separate.
3. Open **Review questions**. Begin with June 2025 1B and 2B. Review each question and subpart against the paper, scheme and report tabs.
4. Check the proposed wording, all choices, shared context and part marks. Inspect complete visual crops, including outside labels and axes; select their proper shared/subpart scope and approve or replace them. Source-label descriptions are drafts you can improve.
5. Check the proposed marking points against the actual scheme table and the complete source-transcription/guidance notes. Native source bullets are not yet validated scoring rules. All parts initially require manual scoring review. Retain any-N caps, accepted alternatives, linked explanations and exclusions when configuring automatic readiness.
6. Use examiner-report pages to inspect commentary and handwritten examples. Candidate answers may be wrong; they must not become approved answers. Report commentary has not been automatically approved or copied as trusted advice.
7. Read proposed textbook matches in the coverage reason. Choose all relevant Biology topics and confirm actual coverage. Hints are not preapproved topic links and do not change any Student's grade/term access.
8. Save and resume drafts as needed. Review confidence/provenance on the dedicated paper accuracy page. Initial extraction triage is conservative and does not establish semantic accuracy.
9. Approve content only after the required checks. Separately verify scoring readiness and complete-paper release. Drawing answers, scheme visuals and optional AI-generated worked answers have their own review requirements.

## Known exceptions

- June 2021 has final and provisional schemes. Only the final schemes are paired for active review. Examiner reports were not supplied; feedback must not claim report advice is available.
- January 2020's source named `PaperBR` is internally identified as 2BR in its paper and scheme. The report cover says 2B, but all six question contexts match 2BR. It is linked with a verified-body/cover-misprint note; see the [identity review](../guidelines/pastpapers/igcse/reviews/2026-11-04-biology-january-2020-2br-report-identity.md). The original cover is preserved.
- An original-paper release needs all questions reconciled and approved. An individual self-contained question can be released to Tutor practice separately when its existing access and review gates pass.

Nothing in this intake automatically approves questions, enables Student papers, changes child coverage or extends a time-limited Tutor pilot.
