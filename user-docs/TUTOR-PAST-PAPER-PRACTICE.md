# Practise past-paper questions with your AKURU Tutor

## Student

Open **Tutor room**, enter an available subject/topic session, and ask:

- “Can you ask me a question from igcse chemistry paper?”
- “What past papers are available?”
- “Discuss 1C question 3(a).” Include the year/session if needed.

AKURU offers only released questions from your enrolled subject and covered, published topics. You do not need the entire textbook or paper to be available. If an item is missing, choose another available question; Admin may still need to review or release it.

Read the question, shared context, marks and diagram. Type **My answer: …** to submit, **Give me a hint** for help, or **Show approved answer** to reveal its approved marking guidance. Follow-up buttons fill the message box; press Send or Enter to send them. Ask **Next question** when finished. Finish or reveal the current item first. **Stop past-paper practice** leaves an unfinished question without awarding marks and lets you return to textbook questions.

Approved marking guidance shows what the exact scheme accepts, plus available reviewed examiner guidance. A diagram labelled **AI-generated** is an Admin-approved worked answer, not a Pearson answer. Expand **Exam source evidence** to view actual source pages. These links are private and cannot be shared to give another Student access.

Revealing an answer before submitting is assisted practice and does not award marks or independent mastery. A saved answer is not necessarily a released score: if automatic marking has not passed AKURU's quality checks, you can compare it with approved guidance but no automatic score is shown. Drawing responses require supported manual review. Your answers and performance belong to your own account.

This chat currently practises one subpart at a time, not a whole timed mock exam. Tutor help is blocked during active formal exams. Practice questions have a one-hour answering window; after expiry, approved guidance is still available and you can request another item.

## Admin

In **Exam documents → Review questions**, finish question and subpart approval. Supported parts need published topic mappings and exact paper/mark-scheme evidence, with reviewed diagrams and a marking-readiness decision.

Expand **Release approved subparts for Tutor chat**. Check that a subpart is self-contained with its shared stem and visuals, then release it and confirm. Do not release a subpart requiring an earlier answer that is not supplied. A full paper need not be published for this individual practice release. The Student must separately have appropriate enrolment, cumulative topic coverage and enabled Tutor/practice access.

Use **Withdraw practice item** to stop access. Editing a reviewed question or source invalidates its prior release; review and release the current version again. Automatic marking quality gates are separate from content approval. Ask the operator to check release configuration if correctly reviewed items are not available to an eligible Student.

Initial production rollout is restricted to the authorized pilot Student. 2C items remain unavailable until approved and explicitly released; unreviewed material is never exposed by this feature.

## Parent

Students use their own accounts. Existing Tutor transcript/summary access and assessment results remain family-scoped. Revealed answers are practice assistance, not proof of independent understanding. A pending or unavailable automatic score does not mean the Student's answer was lost.
