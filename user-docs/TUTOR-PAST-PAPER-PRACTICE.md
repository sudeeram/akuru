# Topic questions and formative feedback in Tutor chat

Updated: 18 November 2026

## Student: original past-paper questions

Open **Tutor room**, choose Biology (or another released subject) and **Subject chat**. Ask, for example:

- “Give me 5 questions from Coordination.”
- “Give me 10 past-paper questions from Topic 6.”
- “Give me three questions from Unit 2.”

Sets contain **1–20 independently answerable subparts**, one at a time. A whole multipart question is not counted as one item. AKURU selects only released, approved material from your enrolled subject and covered published topics. If fewer items are available, it tells you the smaller count; it does not invent original past-paper questions to fill the set.

Read the shared context, question, marks and approved diagrams. Submit in the answer box or type **My answer: …** in chat. AKURU provides an **indicative score**, correct points, mistakes, omissions, a suggested better answer and an explanation. Acknowledge any uncertainty: the score is learning feedback, not a certified exam result. Expand marking guidance and examiner advice to compare with the approved sources.

Choose **Next question** after feedback, reveal or skip; choose **Complete set** after the last item. Progress and submitted responses are saved to your account. Reloading the Tutor room resumes the current set. **Stop set** ends it but preserves your saved history. Finish or stop the current set before starting a new one.

Hints and answer reveals are assisted practice. Skipped/revealed responses are not proof of independent mastery; these indicative sets do not update certified mastery rankings. If feedback fails, your answer remains saved: choose **Retry feedback** rather than submitting a changed answer. No unavailable score is silently treated as zero.

## Student: newly generated questions

Within Subject chat, ask **“Generate 3 new questions from Coordination”** or **“Generate two difficult questions from Topic 6.”** Easy, medium and difficult are requested teaching levels, not measured equivalence to an official paper.

These are labelled **AKURU-generated exam-style questions**, not original Pearson questions or predictions. AKURU uses published topic evidence and approved paper examples for style, creates a hidden suggested answer/rubric and runs an additional content check before serving each new item. Generation happens one question at a time. If checking fails, choose **Retry generation** or use original past-paper practice.

Generated practice initially supports text and calculations. It does not create images or pretend that an unavailable diagram exists. Generated questions remain private to your account/session; they are not automatically added to the approved shared paper bank.

## Existing individual-question practice

You can still ask **“Discuss June 2025 2BR question 2(a)”** or ask for a single available paper question. Existing individual cards remain supported. New individual practice also offers formative feedback without requiring certified automatic marking. Existing validated bounded scoring is reused where possible without a model call; unfamiliar written variants can receive indicative AI feedback.

Text practice cannot reliably grade an unseen drawing. AKURU explains this limitation, offers marking guidance/approved answer visuals where available, and does not invent a score for the drawing. Approved AI-generated worked-answer images are explicitly labelled; they are distinct from official scheme images.

## Admin

Complete question wording, exact question/scheme links, source-conflict decisions, required image reviews and curriculum mapping. Human-approved and valid AI-approved content can be released as independently answerable Tutor-practice subparts. **“Manual marking required” and scoring certificates are no longer prerequisites for this learning workflow.** The optional certified-scoring controls are for separate scoring validation, not another content approval task.

The existing final reassessment/auto-release workflow still checks approved/current sources and curriculum. Use **Release approved subparts for Tutor chat** or the authenticated release-retry workflow to enable eligible current content. An explicit withdrawal remains respected. Do not release a subpart depending on an unavailable earlier answer.

Students also need enrolment, coverage and enabled Tutor access. Editing/withdrawing source content can invalidate a release or an existing set; a Student may need to start a new set after the content changes. Full formal-exam publication is separate and is not performed by a Tutor release.

## Privacy, costs and limitations

Sets, answers and feedback are private to each Student. Access and assets are rechecked against current release/coverage; sharing a URL does not grant access. Tutor help remains disabled during an active formal exam. Existing account routing and per-child usage controls apply to model calls. Routine selection/reveal/skip do not require inference; generation/checking and semantic feedback do.

Parents retain existing family-scoped Tutor transcript/summary access. The explicit Admin transcript-retention action also removes sufficiently old private question-set copies, while preserving recently resumed sets. No Student's results are shared with another child or used to assert certified exam accuracy.
