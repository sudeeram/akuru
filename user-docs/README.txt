AKURU USER GUIDES — ADMIN / iGCSE REVISION
===============================================

Start with GETTING-STARTED.txt, then read the guide for your role:

ADMIN-GUIDE.txt: accounts, enrolments, textbooks, units, coverage and papers.
PARENT-GUIDE.txt: your children's progress, assignments and answer reviews.
STUDENT-GUIDE.txt: term practice, mocks, feedback and study plans.
DATA-AND-SAFETY.txt: private data, local accounts and current limitations.

Phase 1 supports iGCSE, Grade 10 and Grade 11, with Term1, Term2 and Term3.
Students retain multiple completed/current Grade + Term combinations. A Grade 10
Term2 student uses both Grade 10 Term1 and Grade 10 Term2 coverage.
Subjects: English, Maths, ICT, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, French, Human Biology.

Only Admin manages accounts, enrolments and learning documents. This supersedes
the earlier guides that assigned course setup and uploads to parents.

Technical requirements: source-code/docs/architecture.md.
