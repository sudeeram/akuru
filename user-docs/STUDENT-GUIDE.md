# AKURU Student Guide

AKURU pages now have stable addresses. You can bookmark your Flashcard library, a permitted deck or your current Flashcard session. Reloading or using the browser Back and Forward buttons keeps you in the routed learning flow. A session link remains private to your account; sharing it does not give another Student access to your cards or progress.

Use your own account. Your work and reviews belong to you and are separate from
other children. After login, check your name, current grade and term. Earlier
completed terms are included in mock selection. Ask your parent or
Admin to correct them if needed.

If you forget your password, ask an Admin for a temporary password. It works
only for signing in and choosing a new password. On the replacement screen,
enter the new password twice; you do not need to enter the temporary one again.
If you know your password and want to change it, open **Account security**. The
change signs out your other devices. Never share your password with another
Student.


## My Subjects And Practice

Open My subjects, choose a subject and an available question. Questions appear
only after Admin approves them and all their required units are covered for
your saved Grade + Term progression. No questions means curriculum setup is incomplete;
it does not mean you should use another account.

Read the whole question and any diagram. Enter your answer and working. Use
Save draft if stopping before submission. You may attach your own handwritten
working as a PDF or image up to 5 MB. This is an answer attachment, not a textbook
upload. Only Admin can add learning documents.

Try first before asking for hints. Submit when ready, then read the feedback.
An exact numeric answer may be checked immediately. Awaiting review means a
parent needs to assess it; it does not automatically mean your answer is wrong.


## Mock Exams

Open Mock exams, choose your subject and Start mock exam. Find a quiet place and
allow 15 minutes for the current local mini mock. Its questions use only covered
units; full-syllabus source papers are not offered as term mocks.

Save answer before moving between questions. The timer keeps running if you
leave the page. Hints and lesson explanations are unavailable during an active
mock. When finished, choose Submit exam and then Submit paper. After time ends,
you can submit already saved answers but cannot save more edits. Unanswered
questions receive zero marks.


## Feedback And Study Plans

Open My progress and select recent work to read feedback and explanations.
Ask which marking point you missed and try the method again independently.
Practice scores are not predicted qualification grades.

Open Study plan and Create my plan, or Refresh my plan after more practice.
Suggestions use your own work and eligible questions. If a subject says it is
awaiting curriculum setup, ask Admin to prepare its units and questions.


## Approved Resources And Sign-Out

Open **Textbooks** in the Student navigation to browse published textbooks for
your enrolled subjects. Choose a textbook, then a Unit or Module and Topic. The
reader shows the reviewed text, an image of each published primary-source page,
and approved diagrams. If a reviewed Visual Reference is included in the published
Topic, choose **Visual reference · original pages** to read its labelled page images.
You can switch between the text and original-page views. The Visual Reference
opens as a flipbook: use Previous and Next, the left and right arrow keys, or a
swipe on a phone. Larger screens show a two-page spread; choose **One page** if
you prefer. Use **Browse pages** for page thumbnails, **Zoom** for details, or
**Full screen** for focused reading. Choose a source if the Topic has more than
one reviewed Visual Reference.
Each Topic displays its published content version and date; the textbook also
shows its published structure version and date. Draft or unpublished Topics do
not appear. Reading may include later Topics, but assessed practice remains
limited to your covered Grade and Term Topics.
Show your working, answer in your own words, and sign out on shared devices.


## Flashcards

Open **Flashcards** and choose a topic available in your current or earlier Grade and Term coverage. The mastery panel belongs only to your account and shows **To evaluate**, **Needs review**, **Good**, and **Mastered**. Coverage shows how much of the deck you have attempted in completed sets. Mastery shows how well you know the evaluated cards.

Choose **Review Flashcards** to practise Easy, Difficult, or Mixed cards. Choose **Difficult Flashcards** to focus on cards that need review and difficult cards you have not tried. Select 20 or 30 cards. If fewer qualify, AKURU creates a smaller set and tells you how many were available.

Try to answer before choosing **Show approved answer**. No rating is selected for you. The approved answer appears first; use **Show explanation** when a separate explanation is available. Exact textbook evidence has its own open and close control, readable paragraphs, a familiar page number, and an approved figure when one is available.

Select Again, Difficult, Good, or Easy to continue. Your choice cannot be edited. You may move back through attempted cards to review them, but you cannot skip an unanswered card. AKURU records mastery only after the whole set is complete. If you leave early, choose **Continue studying** or **Exit and discard attempt**. Discarding removes only that incomplete attempt and keeps all earlier completed results.

A card becomes Mastered after you select Easy in three separately completed sessions in a row. Again places it in Needs Review and resets that streak. A later Difficult result can move a mastered card back to Good or Needs Review. Your results never affect another Student's mastery. Flashcard study uses reviewed cards already stored in AKURU and consumes no AI tokens while you study.


## Step 17: Understanding Feedback
Open My progress, then select a question. Read how each mark was decided,
the evidence from your answer, what went well and the mistakes to fix.
Compare your response with the improved answer and worked explanation.
A result awaiting human review is provisional until your Parent or Admin reviews it.

Unit cards show scores out of 10, confidence, skill dimensions, recent trends
and the latest ten score changes. Follow the approved next steps and their
linked textbook evidence. No score means more assessed evidence is needed.
Use Enlarge diagram to inspect images; Escape closes the enlarged view.
Common equations are formatted mathematically; unusual notation retains its
original source so you can compare it with the original question.


## Step 18: Visual Explanations
Approved visual explanations appear inside My subjects when they relate to a
unit covered in your current or earlier terms. Enlarge an image when helpful
and follow the official source citation shown under it. The textbook, question
and marking scheme remain the authority; a generated illustration is only a
supporting explanation reviewed by your Admin.

## Privacy

AKURU sends only the answer and approved subject context needed for AI feedback. It uses a pseudonymous learner reference rather than your name when calling the provider. Ask your parent or administrator if you want to understand how long answers and uploaded working are kept.

## My Tutors

Open My tutors to build your tutor team. You can create more than one tutor, choose a child-safe AKURU hero, select how the tutor should explain things, and give the tutor your own name. Editing or renaming a tutor keeps earlier versions safely in AKURU. Removing a tutor hides it from your list.

Open Tutor room to begin a practice conversation in one of your covered units. You can change to another tutor or move to another covered unit in the same subject without losing the transcript. Each message is retained with the tutor profile version used at that time. Tutor help is disabled during mock and official assessments.

You can also ask where a concept belongs, for example: **“I have learnt about viruses. Which topic does this belong to?”** AKURU searches the published topics you can access in the selected subject and shows textbook excerpts, page references and matching topics. Choose **Open this topic** to move there in the same conversation. A lookup does not change topics automatically. If no reliable match is found, name the concept more precisely; inaccessible or unpublished topics are not included.


AKURU stores typed messages, structured text Tutor replies and text transcripts of voice turns. It does not store raw voice recordings. Live voice capture is added in a later Tutor step.

For a text conversation, describe what you need and press Enter or choose Send. AKURU selects the teaching approach for each message: for example, “Explain diffusion”, “Ask me three questions about cells”, “Help me without telling me the answer” or “Help me revise”. An explicit request takes priority over your tutor's preferred style. French conversation is restricted to French sessions.

Ask “How can you help me with this subject?” for an introduction, or “Which topics can we study?” for your available topics. These help replies do not use a paid AI call. Subject chat remains subject-wide; any topic selector inside the separate practice or voice tools affects only those tools. Unavailable tools stay hidden or disabled.

AKURU may show relevant approved textbook figures, with captions and source details. It does not generate new images or display arbitrary image links. If no reliable approved figure exists, it can continue explaining in text. References remain collapsible. If a request fails, your typed message remains so you can retry it.

Tutor preferences influence generated explanations: tone, friendliness, enthusiasm, detail and teaching style. Communication character is labelled Playful, Balanced or Structured. Voice speed affects voice only. The preview is illustrative and makes no AI call. Preferences cannot change scientific facts, approved exam wording, marking rules or access permissions. A session retains its selected profile version; switch to the updated tutor version to apply edits. Style preferences are guidance, so responses will not always match them perfectly.

Use Guided practice to start one question selected from your covered active unit. You can ask for staged hints and save your answer before submitting it. You may change tutors while working on the question, but you must submit it before moving to another unit. Submitted answers use the same AKURU assessment service as other practice. Published feedback shows how marks were decided and an improved answer; feedback needing review stays unavailable until that review is complete.

Tutor observations are shown as non-authoritative. They may help you and your parent notice how a tutoring conversation went, but they never change your unit score, next-unit recommendation or study plan.

In Tutor room, choose Find next unit when you want evidence-based advice about what to improve. AKURU compares only your covered units using assessed results, mastery confidence and trends, recurring assessed mistakes, reviewed recommendations, your current study plan and current-term assessment preparation. It shows the reason and activity before anything changes. Choose Move to this unit if you want to follow the advice; AKURU will never move you automatically.

Use Find an exact explanation to search the approved textbook for your active unit. A result shows the textbook title and edition, its printed page label, the physical PDF page and the exact supporting passage. Open cited page or crop displays the private source image after checking your account and session. Show nearby context adds approved passages from the same unit and textbook version. If AKURU says evidence is insufficient, it has not found a reliable source and will not invent a page or quotation.

## Community and your public name

Open **Community → Settings**, or **My profile → Community identity → Manage community profile**. Choose a public name (3–32 letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens or underscores), select any enrolled subjects you want to show, and enable participation. Use a name that does not identify you personally. Public names are unique; your login username stays unchanged. Existing accounts start with participation switched off.

**Discover** shows participating students in your current course, such as iGCSE. You can search public names and optionally filter by visible subjects. You can follow someone in your course even when you study different subjects. Connections continue if either learner later changes course.

**Follow** creates a one-way connection. The recipient gets an in-app notification and can **Follow back**. **Following** and **Followers** show your own connections. Use **Unfollow** to remove an outgoing connection. The top-bar **Feed** button shows unread follow notifications; open Feed to mark individual notifications read or unread. Follow updates refresh periodically. Exam results, marks and learning history are not shared; Community has no messaging, posts or comments.

Other learners see only your public name, avatar and selected subjects. Hiding a subject also removes you from that subject's search results. Switching participation off hides your profile, pauses new follows and suppresses your community notifications. Existing connections stay private and return when you rejoin.

**Block** removes connections in both directions and prevents you from discovering or following each other. Manage blocks under **Blocked learners**; unblocking does not restore connections. **Report** sends a private profile concern to an Admin without disclosing your identity to the reported learner. If an Admin requests a new public name, your community profile stays hidden until you choose a different acceptable name.

## Questions that ask you to draw

Use **Your drawings** to upload a clear photo or PDF for each named subpart. Include required labels and bonds. **View saved drawing** lets you check the file; upload again to replace it before submitting. Drawings cannot be replaced after submission or the time limit.

After submission, an approved diagram answer may appear in practice feedback or assessment results. Use **Enlarge diagram** to inspect details. Your own drawing may still be awaiting review: this is not a zero score and does not lower your mastery. The score appears after review.


## AI-generated worked graph answers

Some released questions may include an approved **AI-generated worked answer — not an official Pearson answer**. During practice, **Show worked answer** opens explanatory steps and graphs if that answer is available. Using it makes that question assisted practice, so it will not increase your independent mastery ranking.

Expand a worked step to see its graph, explanation and limitations. **Graph values** gives a text alternative; **Enlarge diagram** lets you inspect detail. The official marking scheme remains the authority, and a valid hand-drawn best-fit line can differ slightly from the example.

Worked-answer help is disabled during mock exams and official papers. Approved model answers appear only when the existing submission/feedback rules permit them. Not every question has a generated answer.

## Subject chat and Topic chat

In **Tutor room**, choose **Subject chat** to ask across the published, covered
topics released for you in a subject. Choose **Topic chat** for focused learning
in one selected topic. End your current session before starting a different
session type. Existing conversations remain Topic chats.

Subject chat searches relevant passages rather than sending the entire textbook.
Its **Practice / voice focus** selects the topic for those separate tools; it
does not restrict the text conversation. Question releases and assessment coverage
still apply. Tutor help remains unavailable during formal assessments.

Follow-up messages such as "Why does it happen?" or "Explain that more simply"
use recent conversation context, including the tutor's reply. Be specific when
several ideas could fit; AKURU should ask when the reference is ambiguous. Very
old details may need to be repeated. Moving topics in Topic chat starts a fresh
teaching focus; changing tutor profiles retains the conversation.

On the session-start screen, **Your previous conversations** can be filtered by
Subject chat or Topic chat and expanded to read the saved messages.
