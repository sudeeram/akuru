# AKURU Student Guide

AKURU pages now have stable addresses. You can bookmark your Flashcard library, a permitted deck or your current Flashcard session. Reloading or using the browser Back and Forward buttons keeps you in the routed learning flow. A session link remains private to your account; sharing it does not give another Student access to your cards or progress.

Use your own account. Your work and reviews belong to you and are separate from
other children. After login, check your name, current grade and term. Earlier
completed terms are included in mock selection. Ask your parent or
Admin to correct them if needed.


## My Subjects And Practice

Open My subjects, choose a subject and an available question. Questions appear
only after Admin approves them and all their required units are covered for
your saved Grade + Term progression. No questions means curriculum setup is incomplete;
it does not mean you should use another account.

Read the whole question and any diagram. Enter your answer and working. Use
Save draft if stopping before submission. You may attach your own handwritten
working as a PDF or image up to 5 MB. This is an answer attachment, not a textbook
upload. Only Admin can add learning documents.

Try first before asking for hints. Submit when ready, then read the feedback.
An exact numeric answer may be checked immediately. Awaiting review means a
parent needs to assess it; it does not automatically mean your answer is wrong.


## Mock Exams

Open Mock exams, choose your subject and Start mock exam. Find a quiet place and
allow 15 minutes for the current local mini mock. Its questions use only covered
units; full-syllabus source papers are not offered as term mocks.

Save answer before moving between questions. The timer keeps running if you
leave the page. Hints and lesson explanations are unavailable during an active
mock. When finished, choose Submit exam and then Submit paper. After time ends,
you can submit already saved answers but cannot save more edits. Unanswered
questions receive zero marks.


## Feedback And Study Plans

Open My progress and select recent work to read feedback and explanations.
Ask which marking point you missed and try the method again independently.
Practice scores are not predicted qualification grades.

Open Study plan and Create my plan, or Refresh my plan after more practice.
Suggestions use your own work and eligible questions. If a subject says it is
awaiting curriculum setup, ask Admin to prepare its units and questions.


## Approved Resources And Sign-Out

Read approved textbooks and reference material from your subject page. They may
contain later units, but assessed practice remains limited to your covered units.
Show your working, answer in your own words, and sign out on shared devices.


## Flashcards

Open **Flashcards** and choose a topic available in your current or earlier Grade and Term coverage. Choose Quick review for 10 cards, Normal review for 20, Full topic practice, Difficult cards, Due today or Unit mixed practice. A mode is unavailable when you have no matching cards yet.

Try to answer before selecting **Show approved answer**. Compare your idea with the reviewed answer and open the exact textbook source when you want more context. Then choose Again, Difficult, Good or Easy. AKURU uses that rating to decide when and how often you should see the card again. Ratings are private to your account and are learning aids rather than examination marks.

You can leave an unfinished mode and resume it later. Flashcard study uses reviewed cards already stored in AKURU and does not generate new questions while you study.

After revealing an answer, open **Exact textbook source** to read the approved excerpt and its familiar printed page number. If the excerpt refers to a reviewed figure, AKURU displays that figure with its caption and accessible description. **View matching textbook page** opens an image of the corresponding page from the original visual-reference textbook; it does not open technical JSON data.


## Step 17: Understanding Feedback
Open My progress, then select a question. Read how each mark was decided,
the evidence from your answer, what went well and the mistakes to fix.
Compare your response with the improved answer and worked explanation.
A result awaiting human review is provisional until your Parent or Admin reviews it.

Unit cards show scores out of 10, confidence, skill dimensions, recent trends
and the latest ten score changes. Follow the approved next steps and their
linked textbook evidence. No score means more assessed evidence is needed.
Use Enlarge diagram to inspect images; Escape closes the enlarged view.
Common equations are formatted mathematically; unusual notation retains its
original source so you can compare it with the original question.


## Step 18: Visual Explanations
Approved visual explanations appear inside My subjects when they relate to a
unit covered in your current or earlier terms. Enlarge an image when helpful
and follow the official source citation shown under it. The textbook, question
and marking scheme remain the authority; a generated illustration is only a
supporting explanation reviewed by your Admin.

## Privacy

AKURU sends only the answer and approved subject context needed for AI feedback. It uses a pseudonymous learner reference rather than your name when calling the provider. Ask your parent or administrator if you want to understand how long answers and uploaded working are kept.

## My Tutors

Open My tutors to build your tutor team. You can create more than one tutor, choose a child-safe AKURU hero, select how the tutor should explain things, and give the tutor your own name. Editing or renaming a tutor keeps earlier versions safely in AKURU. Removing a tutor hides it from your list.

Open Tutor room to begin a practice conversation in one of your covered units. You can change to another tutor or move to another covered unit in the same subject without losing the transcript. Each message is retained with the tutor profile version used at that time. Tutor help is disabled during mock and official assessments.

AKURU stores typed messages, structured text Tutor replies and text transcripts of voice turns. It does not store raw voice recordings. Live voice capture is added in a later Tutor step.

For a text conversation, choose the teaching approach that matches what you want: an explanation, questions, guided or Socratic practice, revision, exam technique, or French conversation. Type your message and press Enter or choose Send. AKURU can show the approved textbook citation, an approved visual and suggested follow-up questions with its reply. If a request fails, your typed message remains so you can retry it. AKURU will say when it lacks approved evidence instead of inventing a textbook answer.

Use Guided practice to start one question selected from your covered active unit. You can ask for staged hints and save your answer before submitting it. You may change tutors while working on the question, but you must submit it before moving to another unit. Submitted answers use the same AKURU assessment service as other practice. Published feedback shows how marks were decided and an improved answer; feedback needing review stays unavailable until that review is complete.

Tutor observations are shown as non-authoritative. They may help you and your parent notice how a tutoring conversation went, but they never change your unit score, next-unit recommendation or study plan.

In Tutor room, choose Find next unit when you want evidence-based advice about what to improve. AKURU compares only your covered units using assessed results, mastery confidence and trends, recurring assessed mistakes, reviewed recommendations, your current study plan and current-term assessment preparation. It shows the reason and activity before anything changes. Choose Move to this unit if you want to follow the advice; AKURU will never move you automatically.

Use Find an exact explanation to search the approved textbook for your active unit. A result shows the textbook title and edition, its printed page label, the physical PDF page and the exact supporting passage. Open cited page or crop displays the private source image after checking your account and session. Show nearby context adds approved passages from the same unit and textbook version. If AKURU says evidence is insufficient, it has not found a reliable source and will not invent a page or quotation.
