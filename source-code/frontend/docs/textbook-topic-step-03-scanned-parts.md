# Textbook topics Step 3: upload and extraction review

Admin uploads scanned textbook parts from the **Textbook structure** screen, inside the intended topic. One or many PDF, PNG or JPEG files may be selected. AKURU checks filenames against topic codes and titles and asks for confirmation when a filename appears to belong elsewhere.

Uploaded parts then appear in **Documents & textbooks**, where processing progress, retries and specific failures are shown. The extraction review displays the preserved original page, normalized review page and editable extracted blocks together. Admin can correct the printed page label, block type, text, formula or equation LaTeX, and reading order. Source crops remain visible for diagrams, images, tables and equations.

Saving a reviewed block clears that block's review flag. Content remains unavailable to students until the independent publication workflow in Step 4 confirms that all required pages and flagged blocks have been reviewed.
