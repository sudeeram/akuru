## Review and release gates

1. Compare every draft against the rendered question pages, retaining all subpart labels, common stems, data tables, equations, diagrams and question-specific answer instructions. The extracted text still contains PDF boilerplate and is **not suitable as the published prompt** without editing.
2. Convert each exact scheme section into reviewed marking points, alternatives and max marks. A top-level question's single scheme entry must cover all of its subparts. Do not infer a mark from the numerical allocation alone.
3. Inspect the candidate examiner-report pages in the local manifest and attach only comments truly about that question. An introductory mention does not count as question-level guidance.
4. Map every question to one or more **published Chemistry textbook topics**. The current Unit 1 and Unit 2 materials may support some questions, but no whole-paper eligibility can be inferred from two units alone. An original paper stays unavailable until all its questions are within that Student's cumulative coverage. Pearson's specification says Paper 2 may draw from any topic area, including additional bold “C” content.
5. Upload/review/publish each source question paper first, then its exact scheme and report. Verify Paper 1 sessions use 120 minutes/110 marks and Paper 2 sessions use 75 minutes/70 marks; answers stay private during the exam, and results are scoped to the Student.

No production import or Student release is authorized by this worksheet. Each component is a distinct source and must never share a scheme or report.
