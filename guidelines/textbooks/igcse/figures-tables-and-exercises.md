# Figures, tables and textbook questions

Standard: `akuru-igcse-textbook-screenshots` 1.0.0.

## Original visuals

Inventory every figure/reference, including context photographs. Classify relevance rather than silently dropping images. Preserve original image regions instead of generating replacement scientific artwork. A generated explanation or model drawing must have a separate origin and approval path.

Every crop MUST retain essential external labels, arrowheads, legends, units, axes, scales, grids and panel identifiers. Include captions in the crop where practical and also store searchable caption text separately. Solid/liquid/gas labels in particle diagrams and descriptions beside apparatus are part of the figure. Compare every final crop with its full page; a plausible bounding box or OCR rectangle alone is insufficient. Avoid partial adjacent paragraphs at crop edges.

Store source hash, page ID, original pixel dimensions, coordinate system, bounds, crop checksum, figure identifier, caption treatment and accessible description. Do not claim exact OCR-derived coordinates if the region was selected by an operator. Accessible text should explain the figure's supplied information without inventing an unseen result.

Associate figures with applicable teaching blocks and exercise subparts, including cross-page references. A figure shared by several parts can be reused; a figure mentioned only in one subpart must not be attached indiscriminately to every question.

## Tables and graphs

Preserve headers, units, every row/column, decimal precision, footnotes and deliberately blank response/data cells. Store structured cells and keep the original source image/page link. A sequence of correctly recognised numbers is not a verified table.

For graphs, retain the original plot with full scales, series labels and guides. Explicit source-table data may be stored exactly; graph readings must be labelled approximate and distinguished from measured values. A best-fit line need not be straight. Do not fabricate marks, tolerances or a unique correct drawing where none is provided.

## Exercises and level

Preserve the complete question inventory, nested labels, shared stems, word banks, blanks, source tables and figure references. Leaf prompts may repeat inherited context for standalone retrieval, but must retain the group relationship. Never import a question without its required table or figure.

Textbook questions are not official past-paper rubrics. Missing marks and answers remain null. New AI solutions are separate generated proposals, not source-extracted answers. Original exercise pages and private solution content must remain distinct if later used for assessment.

Separate three notions:

1. Source-printed skill labels and difficulty badges, with their visible location and known/unknown legend.
2. AI-inferred task demand, such as recall, explanation, comparison, graph interpretation, calculation or transfer; record the basis and that it is inferred.
3. Official exam level and mark allocation, established against the actual specification and checked papers/schemes, not a textbook badge or guessed GCSE grade.

Do not interpret a badge reading “5” as five marks or Grade 5 without an authoritative legend. Do not infer grade/term coverage from question difficulty. Pearson specification alignment, student eligibility and mock-exam marking rules are separate checks.
