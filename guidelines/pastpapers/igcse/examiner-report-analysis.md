# Examiner-report analysis

Standard: `akuru-igcse-pastpapers` 1.2.0

Last updated: 2026-10-31

## Identify the type of evidence

Keep general question commentary, common misconceptions, candidate-response examples, example-specific examiner comments, and explicitly awarded marks separate. A report is optional and may discuss only selected parts. Record “no commentary located” or “report unavailable” rather than inventing advice.

Resolve headings such as `Question 2(c)` to the question hierarchy. Split guidance for `(i)` and `(ii)` when the wording supports that distinction; otherwise retain it at parent level. Keep general paper advice separately from question-specific guidance.

## Source and completeness checks

Verify subject, session and covered components/variants against report headings; one report can cover several components, so do not assume its filename identifies every included question. Retain exact report version, PDF page and printed label. Distinguish native commentary from embedded scanned candidate responses and apply the appropriate extraction mode to each region.

Keep awarded marks, handwritten examiner annotations and the candidate's own writing distinguishable. A handwriting transcription can have high apparent confidence while losing a minus sign, subscript, superscript, charge or crossed-out correction. Visually inspect those features and preserve uncertain spans with their original crop. Missing evidence stays missing; an absent report must not be confused with an extraction failure in an available report.

## Cross-page examples

An example image and its commentary may occur on different pages. Track headings, reading order, continuation and references to example content. Do not associate comments solely with the nearest image: one page may comment on the preceding example and then introduce another response. Flag ambiguous relationships for the Admin.

For the June 2025 1C reference: Q2(a) spans report PDF pages 8–9; Q2(c) starts on PDF page 10 and continues with examples/comments on later pages. PDF page numbers differ from printed labels.

## Handwriting

Retain the original candidate-response crop with readable resolution. A vision-capable model may propose a transcription for image regions that lack usable text. Keep uncertain spans explicitly marked and show the image beside the editable transcription.

Do not correct the candidate's scientific mistakes during transcription. Preserve crossed-out text, uncertainty, chemical charges and superscripts where relevant. Separate literal transcription from an explanatory summary. Do not infer the mark awarded unless the report explicitly supports it; any calculated estimate must be distinctly labelled and must not replace examiner evidence.

Candidate examples can contain deliberately incorrect answers. They must be stored/retrieved as examples with their review context, never as approved model answers or standalone scoring rules. Include them in teaching only with sufficient explanation to prevent that confusion. Exclude incidental personal information from student-facing copies.

## Useful output

Propose concise advice tied to exact report evidence: what candidates missed, why marks were lost and what a complete answer requires. Preserve the distinction between a direct examiner statement and an AKURU-derived teaching suggestion. Require review before transcription or derived advice is treated as trusted learning content.
