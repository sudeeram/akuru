# Formative learning release policy

Effective: 18 November 2026.

Apply the existing exact-source extraction, confidence/provenance, visual review, curriculum and source-discrepancy standards. Source confidence measures extraction fidelity, not exam-grade accuracy on every Student response. The official marking scheme governs report disagreements; unresolved authoritative-source conflicts still require adjudication.

For **Tutor formative practice**, do not require “Manual marking required”, certified answer recognition or an “all scoring rules checked” assertion before releasing otherwise eligible approved content. Preserve caps, alternatives, combined responses and dependencies faithfully. Human approval or valid guarded AI approval, reliable exact evidence, published curriculum mapping, reviewed visuals and independent answerability remain required.

The final reassessment/guarded release workflow may release eligible parts even when semantic grading has not been certified. Preserve explicit withdrawals. Content approval does not bypass enrolment, coverage, Student audience controls or the prohibition on Tutor help during an active formal assessment.

Feedback is an **indicative AI score** plus correct points, mistakes/omissions, a scheme-grounded better answer and uncertainty. Existing validated deterministic implementations remain useful where applicable; unsupported interpretation is not automatically zero. When a rubric cannot be safely totalled or a drawing cannot be interpreted, provide honest guidance without inventing a score or obligating a human marker.

New AI-generated exam-style questions are distinct from original papers: authorised published topic evidence supplies facts, approved paper examples guide pattern/demand, and checked private generated rubrics supply suggested answers. Never label generated material official Pearson evidence, fabricate source links or automatically add it to the approved question bank.

This policy supersedes mandatory scoring-certification requirements **only for formative learning**. Separate formal-paper publication/certified-scoring functionality and historical proof records are preserved. Future Biology intake should use this policy without repeating completed source reviews solely to unlock feedback.
